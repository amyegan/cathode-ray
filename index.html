Description

Amy is a community builder and software developer with 10+ years of experience building for the web at companies like Vercel and PayPal. she runs live learning sessions, champions open source builders, and generally makes sure the developer community has what it needs to make the web better, faster, and more accessible to everyone.

When she’s not online, you can find her knitting, birding, or strumming a guitar. She brings the same curious, hands-on approach to everything she does, whether she’s untangling a tricky bit of code or a ball of yarn.
